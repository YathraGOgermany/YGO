EXPERIENCE PARIS TOGETHER

Join our trip.
Explore Paris.
Make memories.

Join YathraGo Germany for a memorable weekend journey from Germany to Paris.

We start our journey in Ulm, with pickup options in Stuttgart and Karlsruhe.

We will depart around 11:00 PM on Friday, 4 December, travelling overnight towards Paris.

On Saturday morning, we will arrive in Paris and enjoy a delicious Kerala / South Indian breakfast.

After breakfast, we will explore Paris together and visit the planned attractions.

In the evening, we will enjoy the beautiful night view of the Eiffel Tower before beginning our return journey at around 11:00 PM Saturday night.

We expect to arrive back in Ulm on Sunday morning.

A Malayalam-speaking Malayali guide from Paris and Malayalam-speaking drivers will be part of the journey.
